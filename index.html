Watching for

The nine major allergens are on by default. Gluten covers wheat, barley, rye, oats, spelt and malt. Your picks are saved on this device.

Data sources

Every barcode is checked on Open Food Facts and USDA FoodData Central at the same time. Allergens from both are combined, so if either one flags an allergen, you see it. USDA covers most US packaged foods; its allergens are read from the ingredient list.

USDA lookups use the app's built-in key, which allows about 1,000 lookups an hour. To use a different key from fdc.nal.usda.gov, paste it here. It stays on this device.
